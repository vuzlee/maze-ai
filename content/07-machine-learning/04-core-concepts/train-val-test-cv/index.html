Train/val/test & cross-validation

Split the data wrong and every number after it is meaningless. Three things to remember:

  • three sets, three jobs: fit · choose · report once
  • the split must match the data: by class ratio, by group, by time
  • nothing may be fit on rows it is later scored on — that is data leakage
01

Mental model

Every score must come from rows that played no part in the decision it judges.

1.1Three sets

Train fits the parameters, validation chooses the model, test reports the final score once.

  • A common split is 60 / 20 / 20; with millions of rows, 98 / 1 / 1 is enough.
  • Once you have seen the test score, do not change the model and measure again.

1.2Why validation is not enough

Choosing the best of many configurations on validation also selects for luck.

  • The more configurations you try, the more optimistic the winning validation score.
  • Only a set that was never used to choose gives an unbiased number.
02

Splitting strategies

A random split is only right when rows are independent and alike. Each way real data breaks that has its own split.

2.1k-fold cross-validation

Cut the data into k blocks; each block takes one turn as validation, and the scores are averaged.

CV = 1k Σj=1..kaverage over k rounds scorejscore on held-out block j
  • k = 5 or 10 is the usual default; cost is k trainings.
  • Report the spread across folds too: it says whether the mean can be trusted.
  • Use CV when data is small; with plenty of data, three fixed sets are enough.

2.2Stratified split

Deal the classes out so every fold has the same class ratio.

  • The default for classification, essential when one class is rare.

2.3Group split

All rows of one patient, customer or device go to the same side.

  • Otherwise the model learns to recognise the entity, not the pattern.
  • Remove duplicate rows before splitting for the same reason.

2.4Time series split

Train only on the past and validate on the period right after it.

  • Never shuffle time-ordered data: the model would learn from the future.
03

Data leakage

Leakage is information not available at prediction time slipping into training: great offline score, collapse in production.

3.1Preprocessing before the split

A scaler, imputer or feature selector fit on all rows has already looked at the test rows.

  • Put every step in a Pipeline so each fold refits it on its own train part.
  • Oversampling such as SMOTE belongs inside the fold too.

3.2Features from the future

Ask of every column: is this value known at the moment of prediction?

  • Aggregates such as "total spend" must be cut off at the prediction time.
  • Labels logged by the business process are the usual source — see Supervised, unsupervised & reinforcement.
