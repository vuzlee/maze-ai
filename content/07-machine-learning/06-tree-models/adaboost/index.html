Machine learning · Tree models

AdaBoost

AdaBoost trains one-cut trees one after another, each aimed at the rows the previous ones got wrong, then lets them vote — the fewer a stump's mistakes, the louder its vote.

01

Mental model

Fit a stump, make its mistakes heavier, fit the next stump on the reweighted rows; at the end the stumps vote.

  • The same six customers as Decision tree, with y = +1 for buy and −1 for no.
  • A stump is a depth-1 tree, one question and two leaves: weak alone, each one wrong on 1–3 rows here.
  • Row weights are the only thing passed from one round to the next.
02

Stump

Try every column × threshold; score each cut by the total weight of the rows it gets wrong.

errweighted error of the stump = Σi wirow weights, sum 1 · [ h(xi) ≠ yi ]1 if the stump is wrong
  • Same scan as a decision-tree split, but scored by weighted mistakes instead of Gini. Equal weights at the start, 1/n.
  • The cut that was best in round 1 scores 0.50 in round 2: the weights force a different stump every round.
03

Round update

From the stump's err come two numbers: its vote and the next round's weights.

3.1Vote weight α

The fewer weighted mistakes, the larger the stump's vote; a coin flip gets none.

αvote of this stump = ½ ln 1 − errerrodds of being right
  • err must stay below 0.5; a stump with err 0 would get an infinite vote, which is why the learner must be weak.
  • More than two classes: SAMME uses α = ln((1 − err)/err) + ln(K − 1), so err only has to beat random guessing, 1 − 1/K; scikit-learn's AdaBoostClassifier uses it (for two classes it is the formula above times 2, same vote).

3.2New weights

Wrong rows are multiplied by eα, right rows by e−α, then all are rescaled to sum 1.

winext round ← wi · e−α yi h(xi)Zy·h = +1 right, −1 wrong
  • Z is just the sum that brings the weights back to 1.
  • After every update the wrong rows hold exactly half the weight, so the last stump is now a coin flip.
  • These two rules are what minimising the exponential loss Σ e−yF gives — the bridge to Gradient boosting.
04

Weighted vote

The model is the sum of the signed votes αt ht; its sign is the answer.

F(x)score = Σt αt ht(x)each stump votes ±α , ŷ = sign F(x)buy if positive

4.1Adding up rounds

Stumps that are each wrong somewhere are right together, because they are wrong in different places.

  • Each stump alone is wrong on 1–3 of the six rows; the vote of the three is wrong on none, because they are wrong in different places.
  • decision_function returns F; predict_proba squashes it into a share.

4.2Boundary

Every stump is one step; their sum is a staircase, and its zero crossings are the boundary.

  • Three stumps on one column give a boundary that no single stump can draw: buy, no, buy along age.
  • Stumps on different columns add up to axis-parallel blocks on the plane, like a tree but softer at the edges.
05

Reining it in

Every round makes the training rows fit better; what to watch is how many rounds and what they chase.

5.1Rounds & learning rate

Test error falls, flattens, then creeps back up; a smaller learning rate slows the whole curve.

  • n_estimators (default 50) is the number of rounds; learning_rate multiplies every α.
  • Lower the rate, raise the rounds, and pick the pair by cross-validation; staged_predict scores every round in one pass.

5.2Label noise

A row with a wrong label is wrong every round, so its weight only grows.

  • Exponential loss punishes a confident mistake without limit: the model spends its rounds on broken rows.
  • With noisy labels prefer Random forest or gradient boosting with a gentler loss.
