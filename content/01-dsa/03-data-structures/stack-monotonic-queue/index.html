Stack &
monotonic stack

Only the top is ever touched — and that is enough to turn many O(n²) scans into O(n).

01

Mental model

A pile of plates: the last one in is the first one out.

1.1Last in, first out

append puts on top, pop takes from the top — nothing else is reachable.

1.2Monotonic stack

Before pushing, pop every smaller value — the stack stays sorted by itself.

02

Properties

Everything at the top is O(1); anything deeper costs a walk.

2.1Push

st.append(x) — one new cell on top, O(1).

2.2Pop

st.pop() — the top cell comes off, O(1).

2.3Peek

st[-1] reads the top without removing it, O(1).

2.4Search

Finding a value means walking down from the top — O(n).

2.5Total cost of the monotonic loop

A while inside a for — still O(n): each index is pushed once and popped once.
