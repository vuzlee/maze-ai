Mental model

1.1Stretch and shrink

r stretches the window to the right; l shrinks it from the left when it breaks the rule.

1.2O(n)

Both pointers only move right — at most 2n moves instead of n² subarrays.

Signals: the problem gives a length k · "every subarray of length k" · anagrams, permutations
