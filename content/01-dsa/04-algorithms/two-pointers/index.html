DSA · algorithm

Two pointers

Two indexes move through the array; each step rules out one cell for good and never looks back.

01

Mental model

Each step drops one cell for good, so one pass is enough.

1.1Closing in from both ends

On a sorted list, a sum too big means the right end can never fit; too small means the left end never can.

1.2O(n)

Every step greys out one end cell, so n cells cost at most n steps instead of n²/2 pairs.
