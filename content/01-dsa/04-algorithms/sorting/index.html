Sorting

Put things in order once, and many problems become one easy pass.

01

Mental model

Sorting is rarely the goal — it is the step that makes the rest easy.

1.1Insertion sort

Take the next card and slide it left past every bigger one.

1.2Merge sort

Split down to single cells, then merge sorted halves by taking the smaller front.

1.3O(n²) vs O(n log n)

Comparing every pair is n × n; halving gives log n levels of n.

1.4Stable sort

Equal keys keep their input order.
