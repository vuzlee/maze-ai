DSA — the map

Read this before any other lesson. It answers three questions: what the subject is, what it contains, where to start.

01

Mental model

The whole shelf holds just two things, and they are also the names of the two lesson groups you see on the home page.

  • The first question on a new problem is always how to store it — once that is chosen, the steps almost reveal themselves.
  • Learning a structure means learning which operations it is fast at, not learning how to implement it.
02

What DSA is

The same task has many approaches that all give the right answer — but some finish instantly and some take an hour. DSA is the study of doing it fast.

read front to back1,000,000 looks
open the middle, drop half20 looks

find one number in a phone book of 1 million entriesthe second way needs the book sorted by name

  • Same machine, same answer — the difference is the method, not a faster machine or cleverer code.
  • The second way costs a sort up front. Sorting first so you can search later is the single biggest idea of this whole shelf.
03

Learning order

Follow this order and each lesson adds just one new idea over the previous one.

1 · Big-ORead a problem and know which approach is allowed. Without this you do not know what you are making fast.
2 · Array & string → hash mapThe two storage choices behind most problems. A hash map trades memory for speed and shows up everywhere.
3 · Two pointers → sliding window → binary searchThe three cheapest ways to scan, and the most common group in first rounds.
4 · Stack · heap · tree & BSTThree ways to store for lookup and traversal. Trees open the door to recursion.
5 · Graph → backtrackingTraversing over the situations that can occur. Many problems never say "graph" but are graphs.
6 · Dynamic programmingLast, because it builds on recursion. Also the most consistently hard group.

Alongside all six stages: LeetCode toolkit — bisect, Counter, deque, heapq. Not a stage of its own, but the thing you look up when you catch yourself writing more than needed.
