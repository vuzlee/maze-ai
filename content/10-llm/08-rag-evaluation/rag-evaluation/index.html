RAG evaluation

Correct retrieval does not mean a correct answer — Recall@K measures retrieval, faithfulness measures the answer, two entirely different layers of measurement.

Skeleton lesson — outline settled, content not yet written.

01

The problem

RAG has two layers of failure (wrong retrieval, or correct retrieval but wrong generation) — measure them together and you cannot tell where to fix.

02

Measuring retrieval

Recall@K, Precision@K, MRR, NDCG.

03

Measuring generation

Faithfulness (does it make things up), answer relevance (does it actually answer the question).

04

Context relevance

Did retrieval bring back what was needed, separately from the answer.

05

End-to-end

When to measure the whole pipeline instead of each layer.
