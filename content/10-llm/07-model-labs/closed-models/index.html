Why there is no architecture lesson

The GPT-4 technical report says outright that it does not disclose architecture, data, or training method. Claude has only model cards and system cards. Gemini has reports that reveal part of it.

What can be inferred, and where to stop

04

How to compare properly

Evaluate on your own tasks rather than public leaderboards; and the benchmark contamination trap.

05

Closed model or open model

Decision table: cost, latency, privacy, fine-tunability, legal constraints.
