Autoencoder, VAE & GAN

Three ways to generate data before diffusion, ordered so each fixes the one before.

Skeleton — outline settled, content not yet written.

01

Problem

Generating new data means learning the data distribution, not memorizing individual samples.

02

Autoencoder

The encoder compresses, the decoder reconstructs, the bottleneck forces feature learning — representation learning without labels.

03

Why an autoencoder cannot generate

Reconstruction loss does not constrain the latent space, so decoding an arbitrary point in it yields garbage.

04

VAE — forcing a latent distribution

The encoder outputs a distribution instead of a point; the reparameterization trick lets backprop pass through sampling.

05

ELBO

Reconstruction loss plus KL divergence; the trade-off between the two is why VAE images are often blurry.

06

GAN — two competing networks

Generator versus discriminator in a minimax game; mode collapse and imbalance are the two reasons it is hard to train.

07

Comparing the three and why diffusion won
