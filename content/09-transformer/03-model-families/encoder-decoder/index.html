T5 — encoder-decoder

The original form from the 2017 paper, and still not outdated for tasks with a clear input.

Skeleton — outline settled, content not yet written.

01

Problem it solves

Tasks with one input sequence and an output sequence quite different from it.

02

Core idea

The encoder reads the whole input, the decoder generates output and looks back through cross-attention — one figure.

03

Mechanism

Cross-attention · text-to-text: every task is written as a pair of strings.

04

Objective function

Cross-entropy on the target sequence, with teacher forcing during training.

05

Trade-offs

Separates understanding from generation; in exchange it has more parameters and is more complex than decoder-only.

06

Used in which models

Machine translation, summarization, and models such as T5, BART, Flan-T5.
