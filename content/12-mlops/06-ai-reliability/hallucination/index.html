Hallucination

The model does not know what it does not know — why hallucination is a natural consequence of next-token prediction, not a bug to patch.

Skeleton lesson — outline settled, content not yet written.

01

The problem

The model always generates the most plausible next token, even when it does not know.

02

Why it happens

Training optimizes for fluency, not for "knowing what you don't know".

03

Measuring hallucination

Against ground truth, or against the retrieval context itself (faithfulness).

04

How to reduce it

RAG, grounding, calibration — there is no way to eliminate it completely.
